How I actually work

I'm a backend engineer based in Chennai, two years into building the internal systems a travel company actually runs on. I don't get excited by "can this be built" — I get excited by "can this be built without breaking what 400 people are using right now." Most of what I've shipped has had to survive that test.

Ship without stopping the business

Plato replaced a company's spreadsheets while those spreadsheets were still in daily use — rolled out team by team, never all at once.

Make every number traceable

The deal size engine doesn't just recalculate — it logs why. If Finance questions a number, there's always an answer.

Automate the repetitive, not the risky

Call scoring, stage movement, and refunds run themselves. Production incidents still get a human — mine.

Optimise before it's a fire

Cut API response times 40%+ by fixing slow queries proactively — not after they'd already caused an outage.
