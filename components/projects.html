Plato — Internal CRM

Sep 2024 → Present · Serving CX, Sales, Product, Visa & Finance

The problem

Five different departments — CX, Sales, Product, Visa, Finance — were each running their own version of the truth in spreadsheets. Customer data, trip status, and workflow handoffs lived in scattered sheets with no single source of truth, so information got stale or lost between teams.

⚠ hardest part: shipping fast without stopping the business

There was no maintenance window to build this in isolation — the old spreadsheet workflow had to keep running while Plato was built and rolled out department by department, on a legacy codebase migrated to modern Laravel in parallel.

What shipped

A centralised CRM that replaced the spreadsheet sprawl with one backend — workflow tracking, customer data processing, and operational reporting in a single system, with background jobs automating what used to be manual busywork.

Stream — Single Customer View

Design & Engineering · Unified CX workspace inside Plato

The problem

A customer's story lived in pieces — AI summaries on one page, staged summaries on another, call notes, chats, and trip details scattered across the rest. Every support case meant agents jumping between tabs just to understand what had already happened.

⚠ hardest part: designing one coherent surface for five different data types

This wasn't just a backend build — it needed an information hierarchy that could hold AI-generated summaries, human notes, chats, and structured trip data side by side without burying any of them, and stay legible under real support-load pressure.

What shipped

Designed and built Stream — a single consolidated view that brings AI summaries, staged summaries, call notes, chats, and trip information into one interface, so an agent can understand a customer's full history without leaving the page.

Context switching cut way down — agents work from one screen instead of many.
Agent productivity improved across CX.
Faster, more confident support decisions.

AI Call Intelligence

AI call summarisation & auto stage movement · Plato

The problem

Agents had to manually listen to full call recordings or dig through notes to understand what happened on a call. Separately, sales and CX pipeline stages were updated by hand — which meant delays and stages that didn't match reality.

Two different manual bottlenecks, both slowing down the same pipeline.

What shipped

Built AI-powered call summarisation with structured insights inside Plato, plus an AI-driven workflow that automatically moves sales/CX stages based on call transcription and business rules — so the pipeline updates itself as calls happen.

Manual review effort and stage updates both dropped sharply.
Call documentation became consistent across every agent.
CRM data accuracy and issue resolution speed both improved.

Deal Size Recalculation Engine

Financial accuracy & audit trail · Plato

The problem

Deal size numbers drifted out of sync every time a booking had a cancellation, refund, GST change, or update — leaving Finance and CX without a number they could fully trust, and no clear trail of why a figure had changed.

⚠ hardest part: keeping every recalculation auditable, not just accurate

What shipped

Built and hardened an automatic deal size recalculation engine that re-syncs on every relevant change, and logs an audit reason for each recalculation — so any number can be traced back to exactly why it changed.

Financial accuracy improved across Finance and CX.
Debugging a wrong number went from guesswork to a clear audit trail.
Greater transparency for every team relying on the number.
