AI Call Intelligence AI

OpenAI + Whisper · call summarisation & auto stage movement · Plato

The problem

Agents had to manually listen to full call recordings or dig through notes to understand what happened on a call. Separately, sales and CX pipeline stages were updated by hand — which meant delays, stages that didn't match reality, and decisions made from stale data.

Two different manual bottlenecks, both slowing down the same pipeline — and both costing sales time.

What shipped

Built AI-powered call summarisation (OpenAI + Whisper) with structured insights inside Plato, plus an AI-driven workflow that automatically moves sales/CX stages based on call transcription and business rules — so the pipeline updates itself as calls happen.

No more listening to full recordings — manual review effort dropped sharply.
Sales works from one consistent live pipeline instead of everyone's notes.
Call documentation became uniform across every agent — nothing gets lost between people.

How the pipeline works

A call comes in, and everything downstream happens without a human touching it.

03 · AIStage rules applied

Trippie — AI Pre-Trip Assistant AI

MCP tool wiring · Test Arena · customer-facing, in production

The problem

Trippie is the first thing a customer meets pre-trip — and when it escalated a chat, the handoff was fragile. Escalations failed, responses lagged, and Trip Custodians had no safe way to test how Trippie would behave before it reached a real customer.

⚠ hardest part: changing how an agent wires its tools without breaking live chats

What shipped

Two launches in under a week: a dedicated Test Arena where Trip Custodians safely try Trippie's behaviour before customers see it, and a migration of tool wiring from function calling to MCP — making escalations more reliable and responses faster.

AI Voice Automation AI

OpenAI + Whisper · follow-ups, lead qualification & trip assistance

The problem

Follow-ups and lead qualification ran on manual outbound calls — easy to miss, slow to schedule, and different from one agent to the next. A lead that didn't get called back in time simply went cold.

Repeated, time-sensitive work — exactly the kind that shouldn't depend on whoever is free.

What shipped

Voice AI workflows on OpenAI + Whisper now handle customer follow-ups, lead qualification, and trip-related assistance — so a case keeps moving even when no human is free to pick up the phone.

Follow-ups no longer depend on agent availability — fewer leads go cold.
Every interaction is transcribed and stored — consistent, searchable, auditable.
Agents freed for the conversations that actually need a human.

Plato — Internal CRM

Sep 2024 → Present · Serving CX, Sales, Product, Visa & Finance

The problem

Five different departments — CX, Sales, Product, Visa, Finance — were each running their own version of the truth in spreadsheets. Customer data, trip status, and workflow handoffs lived in scattered sheets with no single source of truth, so information got stale or lost between teams.

⚠ hardest part: shipping fast without stopping the business

There was no maintenance window to build this in isolation — the old spreadsheet workflow had to keep running while Plato was built and rolled out department by department, on a legacy codebase migrated to modern Laravel in parallel.

What shipped

A centralised CRM that replaced the spreadsheet sprawl with one backend — workflow tracking, customer data processing, and operational reporting in a single system, with background jobs and the AI systems above automating what used to be manual busywork.
